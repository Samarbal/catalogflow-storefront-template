# Demo asset register

The images in this repository are demonstration assets. **No commercial rights, model or property releases, or trademark permissions are documented here.** Figma notes that generated projects may incorporate third party assets and that publishers must verify rights. Unsplash generally allows commercial use of its photos, but its license does not grant rights to brands or logos pictured in them. See [Figma guidance](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/31304485164695-Create-a-Figma-Make-file) and [Unsplash terms](https://unsplash.com/terms).
